======================================================================
TEST 01 - APPAREL / FOOTWEAR RETURN POLICY
======================================================================

User:
How long can I return apparel or footwear?

Assistant:
Apparel and footwear can be returned within 30 days of delivery, subject to the applicable return conditions in the policy.

User:
Does the same return period apply to footwear?

Assistant:
Yes. The policy specifies a 30-day return period for both apparel and footwear, subject to the applicable return conditions.
